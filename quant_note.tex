{\large\bfseries Covered Calls Around M\&A Agreement Disclosures}\par
Solo submission \hfill GQH Systematic Trading / Massive 8-K Options\par

\textbf{Hypothesis and mechanism.}
Writing an out-of-the-money call may add more net value to synthetic stock exposure after M\&A agreement filings than on ordinary dates for the same names:
\[
\theta=\mathbb{E}[(CC-S)_{\rm net}\mid\text{event}]
       -\mathbb{E}[(CC-S)_{\rm net}\mid\text{ordinary}]>0.
\]
Strategic-upside demand could support premiums before integration benefits appear [1]. Genuine deal risk and repricing before entry are competing explanations. Covered calls contain equity, volatility and timing exposures [2]. These papers motivate the test, not an established M\&A edge or guaranteed stock rally.

\textbf{Method and provenance.}
V4 uses the static TOP100 and Massive's \texttt{acquisition\_agreement} or \texttt{merger\_agreement} tags, deduplicated by accession; ambiguous filers are skipped. Entry is the next NYSE session close after filing date. Labels do not prove buyer status, control, first announcement or unique transactions. The preceding-session chain selects 90--180 DTE, nearest 120, and approximately 5\% OTM. Synthetic stock is ATM call minus put plus strike discounted at flat 4\% carry. Primary exit is entry plus 21 sessions, capped at expiry.

The screened-buyer hypothesis was committed as \texttt{63664eb} before first IS. V4 changes selection/timing \emph{after IS inspection}; it is a separate revision, not the original preregistration. Historical runs are preserved. IS is 2024--2025; January--August 2026 OOS is pending. Judges alone evaluate their sealed window.

Each option leg costs 5\% of premium per side; doubled costs are reported. This proxies execution. Up to 120 seed-7 ordinary controls lie 30 calendar days from agreement tags, without price-based redrawing. Missing or over-three-session-stale marks remain unavailable.

\textbf{Robustness and falsification.}
Doubled-cost $\theta=0.95$ pp (CI $[-0.39,2.34]$). All 27 close-entry/21-session expiry--OTM--tag point estimates are positive (0.20--1.83 pp); all-issuer leave-one-out estimates are 0.74--1.06 pp. Neither establishes independence: 15 same-issuer windows overlap. Pre-entry implied/absolute-realized medians are 7.14/4.15\% (43 events) and 7.84/4.39\% (87 controls). This yardstick does not prove call mispricing. All 972 correlated grid cells are retained; pre/filing-day views remain diagnostic. Nonpositive $\theta$ refutes the prediction; zero-crossing intervals are inconclusive. No OOS retuning.

\textbf{Capital, risk and trading (partial ledger).}
The \$1 million ledger uses one contract per signal, one concurrent position per name, zero cash return and no leverage. Stock-reference limits: name/industry/total 10/25/100\%; pause/resume drawdown 10/5\%, using prior-session state. The inherited 14-name industry map makes this a different sample from the event comparison. Of 13 commitments, 11 close with priced entries/exits, one entry is unpriced and one exit unresolved.
