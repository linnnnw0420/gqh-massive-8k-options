# References and permitted inferences

The sources below motivate the experiment and its safeguards. None establishes that buyer agreement disclosures create a profitable covered-call signal. The original hypothesis remains in `HYPOTHESIS.md`; references do not retrospectively turn a tested conjecture into an established fact.

## Economic mechanism and counterarguments

1. Israelov, R., and Nielsen, L. N. (2015). Covered calls uncovered. *Financial Analysts Journal*, 71(6), 44–57. https://doi.org/10.2469/faj.v71.n6.1. [Author manuscript](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/Journal-Article/Covered-Calls-Uncovered.pdf). Covered-call performance mixes equity, short-volatility and timing exposures. This motivates the stock-relative event-minus-ordinary estimand; it does not establish an M&A premium. The authors are affiliated with AQR.
2. Gârleanu, N., Pedersen, L. H., and Poteshman, A. M. (2009). Demand-based option pricing. *Review of Financial Studies*, 22(10), 4259–4299. https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhp005. [Author manuscript](https://www.haas.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/DBOP.pdf). Demand and constrained intermediation can affect option prices. The sign and persistence of demand after a buyer's agreement announcement remain unverified here.
3. Ben-Rephael, A., Da, Z., Easton, P. D., and Israelsen, R. D. (2022). Who pays attention to SEC Form 8-K? *The Accounting Review*, 97(5), 59–88. https://doi.org/10.2308/TAR-2019-0425. [Author manuscript](https://www.lehigh.edu/~jms408/Israelsen_2018.pdf). Disclosure, attention and filing clocks must be distinguished. This is general 8-K evidence, not proof of post-agreement option returns.
4. Christoffersen, P., Goyenko, R., Jacobs, K., and Karoui, M. (2018). Illiquidity premia in the equity options market. *Review of Financial Studies*, 31(3), 811–851. https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhx113. Liquidity affects measured option returns; indicative marks and fixed premium haircuts do not prove executable profits.
5. Bargeron, L. L., Lehn, K., Moeller, S. B., and Schlingemann, F. P. (2014). Disagreement and the informativeness of stock returns: The case of acquisition announcements. *Journal of Corporate Finance*, 25, 155–172. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcorpfin.2013.11.014. Announcement uncertainty can persist or change; a signed agreement does not guarantee falling uncertainty or buyer upside.
6. Chan, K., Ge, L., and Lin, T.-C. (2015). Informational content of options trading on acquirer announcement return. *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis*, 50(5), 1057–1082. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022109015000484. Pre-announcement option information does not demonstrate a tradable edge after our filing-based entry.

## Rules, clocks and data semantics

- [GQH Systematic Trading](https://www.gqhacks.com/tracks/systematic-trading) and [Massive subtrack](https://www.gqhacks.com/tracks/systematic-trading/massive). Project-specific interpretation, conflicts, verification dates and current readiness are maintained in `RULES.md`.
- [SEC Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/forms/form8-k.pdf) and [EDGAR FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions). SEC acceptance is an operational proxy, not verified exact public dissemination time; earlier issuer news can already be priced.
- [Massive option quotes](https://massive.com/docs/rest/options/trades-quotes/quotes), [options overview](https://massive.com/docs/rest/options/overview) and [NBBO explanation](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/does-massive-offer-level-2-data). Standing consolidated quote states differ from recent updates and executable simultaneous fills. REST condition/control coverage and historical feed completeness remain limitations.
- [OPRA Pillar output specification](https://cdn.opraplan.com/documents/OPRA_Pillar_Output_Specification.pdf). Consolidated quote messaging informs valuation semantics. Provider-normalized size units remain unresolved in this implementation; no depth-based capacity is inferred.
- [Massive 8-K text](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/filings/8-k-text) and [filing index](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/filings/index). Available core-item text and index metadata do not establish complete exhibit coverage or exact acceptance clocks for the automated unseen-window pipeline.
- [Massive taxonomy product description, July 22, 2026](https://massive.com/blog/tagging-8-k-disclosures-with-ai-corporate-events-labelled-by-what-actually-happened). Vendor retrospective labels are not proven point-in-time 2024–2025 signals. Vendor claims do not independently establish classification accuracy or exhaustive market coverage.

The literature is contextual evidence, not an additional data-mined signal. No direction, horizon, strike or eligible event is selected from these papers' reported returns. Source summaries are bounded by the material reviewed; no unsupported full-text claim is made.
